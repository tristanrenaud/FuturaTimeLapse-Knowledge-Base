# Use cases

| Use case | Typical shake | Recommended settings |
|---|---|---|
| Construction & civil engineering | Offsets after each intervention, mount drift | Alignment area on neighbouring buildings/roads, mask cranes and the structure, blur detection, dates on video |
| Landscape & mountains | Wind jitter, moving clouds | Shift only, ridgeline/foreground as alignment area, blending |
| Seasons & handheld series | Large shifts, tilt, angle changes | Perspective, manual alignment for stubborn frames, choose image shape |
| Science & research | Small offsets spoiling measurements | Save aligned images (not only video), mask, alignment area |
| Sky & eclipses | Tracking drift, low detail | Low-texture alignment, alignment area, blending |
| Cities & everyday life | Traffic vibration, passers-by | Mask moving elements, blending, slow zoom |
