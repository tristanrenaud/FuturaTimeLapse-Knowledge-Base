# Shooting for easy alignment

General practices that make alignment more reliable (contributions and corrections welcome).

- Keep **fixed, high-contrast features** in the frame (buildings, ridgelines, posts) that will not move during the whole sequence.
- Use a solid tripod; weigh it down in wind.
- Lock focus (manual) and use manual exposure/white balance when possible.
- Shoot a bit wider than your final framing: alignment and shape changes crop the edges.
- For repeat visits, mark the tripod position and note the camera/lens settings.
- Keep file names or EXIF dates consistent so the sequence sorts by date.
- Remove obviously blurry or badly exposed photos, or let the app flag them.
- Free Community Edition processes up to 100 images per analysis; plan splits for longer sequences.
